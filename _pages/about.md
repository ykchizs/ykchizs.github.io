I am Yankuan Chi, a master in computer science student at the University of California San Diego. Previously, I obtained my bachelor degree in computer science and mathematics at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. I was an exchange student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in spring 2025.

My research interests lie in computer vision, with a particular focus on 3D vision and 3D-aware visual generation. I am currently fortunate to work in Prof. [Truong Nguyen](https://sites.google.com/view/ucsdvpl/?_gl=1*obp7lp*_gcl_au*MTM5MDY0OTE1OC4xNzg4MDYzMTYwLi0uLS4xNzg4MDYzMTYwLjk3NDU3MDQ0NC4xNzkyMzQ1OTc4LjE3OTIzNDU5Nzg.*FPAU*MTM5MDY0OTE1OC4xNzg4MDYzMTYw*_ga*NTQyNzExMDEyLjE3NjEwNTk3Mzc.*_ga_PWJGRGMV0T*czE3OTIzNDU5NzckbzgzJGcwJHQxNzkyMzQ1OTk4JGozOSRsMCRoMA..)’s lab at UCSD. Previously, I was grateful for the opportunity to conduct research under the supervision of Prof. [Gary Chan](https://www.cse.ust.hk/~gchan/) at HKUST and Prof. [James Rehg](https://rehg.org/) at UIUC.
